## Getting Started

### Do I need to have built an app before?

No. If you can use a laptop, install software, and are curious about how things work, you belong here. We start from the very beginning.

### What if I have never used Kotlin or Android Studio?

That is exactly what Week 1 is for. We teach Kotlin from scratch and walk through Android Studio setup together. Every member gets to the same starting line.

### How much time does the track take?

About 8–10 hours per week: one 90-minute session plus 1–2 hours for the take-home challenge. You can spread it however you like.

### Do I need a powerful laptop?

Android Studio runs on most modern laptops. If your laptop is slow, you can use the Android emulator or test on a physical device instead. Let us know in the track channel and we can help.

## Learning and content

### What if I miss a session?

No problem. The week's code, notes, and challenge are all in this repo. Work through the folder at your own pace and ask questions in the track channel.

### Can I work ahead?

Absolutely. If you finish early, open the next week's folder and explore. Ask in the channel if you have questions.

### What if I don't understand something?

Ask. No question is too basic. We have a help channel in the track and pair programming opportunities during sessions.

### Why Kotlin and Compose instead of Java and XML?

Kotlin is modern, safer, and more fun to write. Jetpack Compose is how Google is building Android UIs now. Learning the current tools means you can contribute to real projects sooner.

## Projects and capstone

### What should my capstone project be?

Something you actually want to build, not something you think sounds impressive. A study planner, a local resource finder, a habit tracker—anything that helps a real user or solves a real problem.

### Can I build with teammates?

Yes. Teams of 2–3 are great. Bigger teams are harder to coordinate.

### What if my idea is too big?

Scope it down. A small, finished app is better than a half-finished monster. You can always add features after demo day.

### Can I showcase my project after the track ends?

Yes, please. We can share it in the chapter channel, on your GitHub, or present it at a future GDG event.

## Community and roles

### How do I become a maintainer or mentor?

Ask the track lead or open an issue in [core-team-discussions](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/core-team-discussions). We look for members who are active, kind, and willing to help others.

### Can I contribute to this repo?

Yes. Fix typos, add resources, share your notes, improve examples. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](../CONTRIBUTING.md).

### What if I want to help run a session?

Talk to the track lead. We always need help explaining concepts, debugging, and cheering people on.

## Technical issues

### Android Studio keeps crashing. What do I do?

1. Try restarting it
2. Check that you have enough disk space
3. Ask in the track channel with your laptop specs
4. We can help troubleshoot or find an alternative setup

### The emulator is too slow.

Use a physical Android device if you have one. The emulator gets faster if you give Android Studio more memory. Ask in the channel for tuning tips.

### Firebase won't connect.

Check that your config file is in the right place and your internet connection is stable. Week 5 has a Firebase setup checklist.

## Other questions?

Open an issue or ask in the track channel. We are here to help.
